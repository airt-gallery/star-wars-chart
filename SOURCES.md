Flagged on the chart:
- Sizes are rounded; reference books sometimes disagree (for example the Tantive IV), and the Death Star's width is left out because sources differ (note 6).
- Boba Fett's ship was long called Slave I; newer stories mostly call it his Firespray (note 5).
- Kamino sits outside the usual galaxy map; the chart says "far beyond the Outer Rim". Jakku is in the Inner Rim, in the area called the Western Reaches.
- The ring diagram on page 1 shows region only, not real positions.
