# Star Wars planet and ship chart

A free, printable who's who of well-known Star Wars planets and ships: 9 pages, A4 landscape. 24 planets colour-coded by what the land is like, and 29 ships colour-coded by who flies them, with a ring map of the galaxy, a who-flew-what table, a size check and notes.

Built by MSTR BLDR on **AiRT Official**. An unofficial fan reference, written fresh in our own words.

## Pages

1. Front page: colour keys, era tags, contents and a ring map of where the planets sit
2. Desert, rock and fire worlds
3. Green worlds
4. Ocean, ice and city worlds
5. Heroes' ships
6. Clone Wars-era ships
7. Empire and First Order ships
8. Quick reference: who flew what, planet finder, size check
9. Notes and sources

## How to use

**View it free:** https://airt-gallery.github.io/star-wars-chart/

**Download the PDF:** [star-wars-planet-ship-chart.pdf](https://airt-gallery.github.io/star-wars-chart/star-wars-planet-ship-chart.pdf)

Or download the files from this repo and print the PDF at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour. You can also open `star-wars-planet-ship-chart.html` in a modern browser and print from there.

## Files

- `star-wars-planet-ship-chart.pdf`: the chart. Print at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour.
- `star-wars-planet-ship-chart.html`: the source. Chrome lays out the cards, checks nothing spills off a page, gets cut off or overlaps, and prints the PDF. The finished PDF has no scripts, links or form fields; fonts are embedded (Exo 2 and Barlow, both open-licence fonts).
- `index.html`: the landing page with a page-by-page preview (`pages/`).

## Checks

- Every page rendered and looked at; an automatic check finds any card that spills past the page edge, into the footer, is clipped or overlaps another (0 found).
- Names, regions, ship types and sizes checked against Wookieepedia and the official reference books it cites (see `SOURCES.md`). Sizes are rounded, and books sometimes disagree.
- All wording is our own: no lines from the films, series or books, and no film stills, logos, official artwork or official fonts.

## The ASTARR prompt used

PROBLEM: My kids love Star Wars, but we can never remember which planet is which, or who flies which ship.

ASTARR PROMPT

ACT AS: A Star Wars fan historian and information designer who makes clear, printable charts.

SPECIFIC TOPIC: A who's-who chart of well-known Star Wars planets and ships, for families watching the films and series together. Write it all in your own words: no quotes from the films or books, and no film stills, logos, official artwork or official fonts.

ACTIONS: Pick about two dozen planets and about thirty ships that families will spot on screen. Give each one a card: where it is, what it's known for, who you meet there or who flies it, and the era it turns up in. Colour-code planets by what the land is like and ships by who flies them. Add a page showing where the planets sit in the galaxy, a quick-reference page and notes at the back. Check every name, fact and size twice against trusted sources.

RESULT&RULES: I want a printable A4 landscape PDF of about 9 pages, with a simple colour key on every page. Easy to read, family-friendly, and marked as an unofficial fan reference.

ASTARR = Act As, Specific Topic, Actions, Result, Rules.
