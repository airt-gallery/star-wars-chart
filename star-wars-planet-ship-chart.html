Tatooine

Desert & rock
Where: Outer Rim

  • Mos Eisley spaceport and the podrace track at Mos Espa
  • Crime boss Jabba the Hutt runs his gang from a desert palace

Desert & rock
Where: Inner Rim (Western Reaches)

  • The wrecks come from a last big battle between the New Republic and the Empire
  • Scavengers trade salvage for food at Niima Outpost

Jedha

Desert & rock
Where: Mid Rim

Who you'll meet: Saw Gerrera, Chirrut Îmwe and Baze Malbus

Desert & rock
Where: Outer Rim
  • White salt flats over bright red soil

  • An old Rebel base becomes the Resistance's last stand

Notes and sources

1Eras

The coloured tags on each card show when a planet or ship turns up on screen: Republic era (the Clone Wars), Empire era (the Rebellion), New Republic era (after the Empire falls) and First Order era (a generation later). Some places turn up in more than one.

2Regions

The galaxy is often described in rings, from the crowded Core Worlds in the middle out to the wild Outer Rim and the barely charted Unknown Regions. Kamino sits outside the usual map altogether. The ring diagram on page 1 shows region only, not real positions.

3Moons, not planets

Endor's forest moon, Yavin 4 and Jedha are moons. Endor is the name of the gas giant they circle; the Ewoks live on its forest moon.

4Same ship, many owners

The Millennium Falcon passes from Lando to Han Solo and Chewbacca, is lost for years, and is found on Jakku by Rey and Finn, who fly it away.

5Names that changed

Boba Fett's ship was called Slave I for many years; newer stories mostly just call it his Firespray gunship. Kylo Ren was born Ben Solo, the son of Han Solo and Leia Organa.

6Sizes

Ship sizes come from official reference books and fan encyclopedias, and some books disagree. Treat them as rounded guides. The Death Star is described as moon-sized; we leave out an exact width because sources differ.

7Destroyed worlds

Alderaan is destroyed by the first Death Star, and Jedha's holy city by its first test. Starkiller Base itself is destroyed by the Resistance. Mandalore's surface is bombed to glass by the Empire.

8What's left out

There are hundreds of planets and ships in films, series, books and games. This chart picks well-known ones a family is likely to spot on screen. It is not a complete list.

How this chart was made

Built with Ai on AiRT Official from one ASTARR prompt, then checked by hand. Every planet, ship, pilot and size was checked against the sources below. All wording is our own: no lines from the films, series or books, and no film images, logos, official artwork or official fonts.

Sources (checked 24 Nov 2026)

  • The Star Wars films and TV series (on-screen events)
  • Wookieepedia, the Star Wars fan encyclopedia (planet regions, ship classes, makers and sizes), which cites the official reference books and the StarWars.com Databank
